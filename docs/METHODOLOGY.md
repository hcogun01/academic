# Detailed Methodology

## Study Title
Effects of Cadmium Chloride (CdCl₂) on Blood Tissue Biochemical Parameters and Liver Antioxidant Levels in Mice Exposed to Cadmium Chloride

## Institution
Çukurova University Health Sciences Experimental Animal Research Center (SABİDAM)

## Ethical Approval
- Conducted according to institutional animal care and use protocols
- Approved by Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee (IACUC)
- All procedures followed humane animal research standards

## Experimental Model

### Subject Selection
- **Species:** Swiss albino mice
- **Strain:** Swiss albino
- **Age:** Adult (age at start of experiment)
- **Sex:** Not specified (recommend documenting)
- **Body Weight:** 30±1 g
- **Source:** SABİDAM

### Justification for Model
Mice are ideal for this research because:
- Genetic similarity to humans (~95%)
- Well-characterized physiology and genetics
- Rapid reproduction and growth
- Easy maintenance in laboratory environment
- Extensive literature for comparative studies
- Established protocols for cadmium toxicity research

### Housing and Husbandry
- **Housing:** Plastic cages (30×15×30 cm)
- **Group size:** 2-3 mice per cage
- **Bedding:** Straw (changed every 3 days)
- **Temperature:** 25±1°C
- **Light cycle:** 8 hours light / 16 hours dark
- **Feeding:** Standard mouse chow (1.5 g/mouse/day)
- **Water:** Ad libitum access to distilled water
- **Ventilation:** As per institutional standards

### Acclimatization
- Duration: Until average weight reached 30±1 g
- Purpose: Allow adaptation to laboratory conditions
- Monitoring: Daily observation for abnormalities

## Experimental Design

### Study Type
- Experimental animal study (controlled intervention)
- Between-subjects design
- Single time-point measurement (Day 10)

### Sample Size and Randomization
- **Total animals:** 18 Swiss albino mice
- **Group allocation:**
  - Group I (Control): n=6
  - Group II (Low-dose): n=6
  - Group III (High-dose): n=6
- **Replicate structure:** 2 mice × 3 replicates per group
- **Randomization:** Random assignment to groups and cages

### Experimental Groups

#### Group I: Control (n=6)
- Normal diet
- No chemical administration
- Maintenance period: 10 days

#### Group II: Low-dose CdCl₂ (n=6)
- Intraperitoneal (IP) injection: 0.1 mg/kg/day
- Duration: 10 consecutive days
- Vehicle: Physiological saline or appropriate solvent
- Administration: Same time each morning

#### Group III: High-dose CdCl₂ (n=6)
- Intraperitoneal (IP) injection: 1.0 mg/kg/day
- Duration: 10 consecutive days
- Vehicle: Physiological saline or appropriate solvent
- Administration: Same time each morning

### Dose Selection Rationale
- **LD₅₀ for Cd in mice:** 8.5 mg/kg
- **Low-dose:** 0.1 mg/kg = 1.2% of LD₅₀
- **High-dose:** 1.0 mg/kg = 11.8% of LD₅₀
- **Rationale:** Selected to produce measurable effects without lethality

## Cadmium Chloride Administration

### Chemical Specifications
- **Chemical:** Cadmium chloride hexahydrate (CdCl₂·6H₂O)
- **Supplier:** Sigma (or equivalent)
- **Purity:** ≥ 99%
- **Storage:** As per manufacturer recommendations

### Preparation
- **Stock solution:** Prepared at appropriate concentration
- **Solvent:** Physiological saline (0.9% NaCl)
- **pH:** Adjusted to physiological range (6.5-7.5)
- **Sterilization:** Filter sterilized before use
- **Storage:** Stored at 4°C for duration of study

### Administration Protocol
- **Route:** Intraperitoneal (IP) injection
- **Volume:** Standardized per body weight
- **Time of injection:** 9:00 AM daily (consistent timing)
- **Duration:** Days 0-10 (10 consecutive days)
- **Operator:** Trained personnel
- **Documentation:** Record injection date, time, dose, and animal response

## Tissue Collection

### Timing
- **Collection day:** Day 10 (immediately after final injection)
- **Timing after final injection:** Within 1-2 hours

### Anesthesia
- **Agent:** Ketamine
- **Dose:** As per institutional protocols
- **Verification:** Confirmation of deep anesthesia before proceeding

### Tissue Harvesting
1. Surgical field disinfection
2. Midline abdominal incision
3. Careful liver exposure
4. Rapid tissue removal
5. Placement on ice immediately

### Tissue Processing
- **Rinsing:** 0.9% physiological saline
- **Storage:** Eppendorf tubes
- **Temperature:** Maintained on ice
- **Freezing:** -80°C freezer
- **Duration to freezing:** < 5 minutes

### Blood Collection
- Collected during tissue harvesting
- Centrifuged to obtain plasma
- Stored appropriately until analysis

## Enzyme Analysis

### Laboratory Facility
- Analysis performed at: Mega Tıp (Gaziantep, Turkey)
- Methods: Spectrophotometric ELISA

### Parameters Analyzed

**Liver Tissue Enzymes:**
1. Malondialdehyde (MDA)
2. Glutathione Peroxidase (GPx)
3. Thiobarbituric Acid Reactive Substances (TBARS)
4. Lipid Peroxidation (LP)
5. Catalase (CAT)
6. Reduced Glutathione (GSH)
7. Total Antioxidant Status (TAS)
8. Total Oxidative Stress (TOS)

**Blood Plasma Parameters:**
1. Alanine Aminotransferase (ALT)
2. Aspartate Aminotransferase (AST)
3. Glucose
4. Cortisol

### Analysis Methods
- Spectrophotometric methods
- ELISA-based assays
- Commercial kits or established protocols
- Protein normalization (Bradford/Lowry method)

- One-way ANOVA
- Student-Newman-Keuls (SNK) post-hoc test
- Linear regression for dose-response analysis
- Significance level: P < 0.05

- Signs of severe distress
- Inability to access food/water
- Signs of organ dysfunction

## Limitations
- Single time point (no temporal dynamics)
- Two dose levels (limited dose-response characterization)
- Limited duration of exposure (10 days)
- Small sample size (n=6 per group)
